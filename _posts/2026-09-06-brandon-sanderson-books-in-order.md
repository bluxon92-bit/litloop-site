## What Is the Cosmere?

The Cosmere is the shared universe behind most of Brandon Sanderson's adult fantasy. Different series take place on different planets, all connected by a common cosmology and a magic system built on something called Investiture.

You don't need to understand any of that to enjoy a single series on its own - Mistborn works perfectly well by itself - but the connections become increasingly rewarding the more of it you read. Nothing is fully gated behind another series; the crossovers are bonuses, not homework.

## Series-by-Series Breakdown

### Mistborn Era 1

Set on Scadrial, centred on Allomancy - a magic system where swallowed metals are "burned" to grant powers. Complete trilogy, no cliffhanger between books.

- The Final Empire
- The Well of Ascension
- The Hero of Ages

### Mistborn Era 2

Same world, 300 years later, now with guns, trains, and a noir-adventure tone.

- The Alloy of Law

Set on Roshar. Planned as two five-book arcs; the first is now finished.

- Rhythm of War

Each can be read on its own with no prior Cosmere knowledge required.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What order should I read Brandon Sanderson's books in?**

Start with Mistborn Era 1, then move to the Stormlight Archive. It's the order that gives you a complete, satisfying trilogy first before committing to the larger series, and it's the order most first-time readers are pointed toward.

**Do I need to read the whole Cosmere before starting the Stormlight Archive?**

No. The Stormlight Archive is written to stand on its own. There are connections to other Cosmere books, but nothing is required reading - they're rewarding extras for attentive readers, not prerequisites.

**Is the Stormlight Archive finished?**

The first five-book arc is complete as of *Wind and Truth* (December 2024).

Sanderson has described the series as eventually running to two five-book arcs, so more is planned, but arc one now has a proper ending.

**Do I need to read the novellas (Edgedancer, Dawnshard, The Emperor's Soul)?**

They're optional, but worth it if you're reading closely. Edgedancer and Dawnshard both feed directly into later Stormlight books, and The Emperor's Soul (a Hugo Award winner) is a strong standalone in its own right.

**What's Brandon Sanderson writing outside the Cosmere?**

Quite a lot, as of 2026. *Songs of the Dead* launches a new non-Cosmere series called The Strata Wars, and *Blightfall* kicks off Riftwake, a YA-leaning space fantasy set in his existing Skyward universe.

Neither is required reading for the Cosmere, but both are worth knowing about if you're trying to keep up with everything he's releasing.
